# Privacy and Safety Safeguards

1. **Synthetic data only.** All 16,934 transactions are generated by `src/generate_data.py`. No real customers, accounts, devices or bank records are used.
2. **Masked identifiers.** Users (U0001-U0300), beneficiaries (B0001-B0615) and devices are generated placeholders with no link to real people.
3. **No connection to payment systems.** The prototype never contacts UPI, NPCI or any bank and cannot initiate, block or reverse a transaction.
4. **Read-only analytics.** The system scores and explains transactions. A human analyst makes every decision.
5. **No label leakage.** `is_fraud` and `scenario` are used only for training targets and evaluation, never as model features.
6. **Time-based evaluation.** The model trains on the first 70% of transactions and is tested on the last 30%, so it is never evaluated on data from its own past.
7. **Local case storage.** Analyst notes are stored in SQLite (`data/cases.db`), which is excluded from version control.
8. **Explainability.** Every score comes with human-readable reasons so decisions can be audited.
9. **Limitations.** Synthetic data is cleaner than real UPI traffic. Real deployment would need real labelled data, consent and governance, drift monitoring and analyst feedback loops.
